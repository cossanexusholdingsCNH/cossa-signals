What we've actually found

RDBULL / RDBEAR — Daily Reset Reversion

One real, explainable pattern — not yet a tradeable product

Deriv resets these two "Daily Reset" indices to a baseline every 00:00 GMT. Direct measurement against 180 days of 5-minute data confirmed this isn't noise: the overnight gap at reset is roughly 10x the size of a normal candle-to-candle move — a scheduled, designed event, not randomness.

A purpose-built strategy trading that exact window — rather than a generic indicator stumbling into it — was tested with a 70/30 train/test split. It held up on data it never saw during development, which is the bar for "worth watching," not the bar for "worth trading."
